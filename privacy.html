Privacy policy

MieszkankoAI · personal and private use

MieszkankoAI is used privately by one household user. It processes Google data only to carry out an operation requested by that user.

Google data access

Where configured, Gmail and Google Drive are accessed in read-only mode. Google Calendar may be used to create an event only when the user explicitly requests that action. The application does not claim or require access beyond the services and permissions configured for it.

Use and storage

Data is processed only for the requested assistant operation and is not sold or shared with other people or services. OAuth tokens are stored on the household’s private homelab, not in a public repository.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the household administrator through the configured MieszkankoAI support channel.
