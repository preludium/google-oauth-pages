Private household integration

MieszkankoAI

MieszkankoAI is a private Google Workspace integration for one household user. It connects to Google services only when needed to provide assistant functionality requested by that user.

  • Google Calendar for calendar-related assistant requests.
  • Gmail and Google Drive for the requested assistant operations, with access limited to what is configured.

This is a personal, private application—not a public service or a product for general users.
